About Crypto Tracker

Welcome to Crypto Tracker, your lightweight application for monitoring live cryptocurrency market data and details.

Features

  • Market Overview: View top cryptocurrencies live with prices and market caps formatted in Indian Rupees (INR).
  • Detailed Search: Look up individual coins by their unique ID (e.g., bitcoin, ethereum, solana) to view descriptive information, current pricing, and market statistics.

Data Source

This application fetches real-time public data directly from the CoinGecko API.
